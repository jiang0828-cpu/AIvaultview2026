Article 111(4), exact excerpt:
Providers of AI systems, including general-purpose AI systems, generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content, that have been placed on the market before 2 August 2026 shall take the necessary steps in order to comply with Article 50(2) by 2 December 2026.

Research scope note (editorial paraphrase): Article 50 distinguishes provider duties concerning machine-readable marking from deployer disclosure of deep fakes and relevant public-interest text. Different exceptions and conditions apply. The quoted transition is for the specified earlier systems and Article 50(2); it is not a postponement of every transparency duty.
