## Reader verdict (cold read of `verification/world-evidence-176.md` and `-185.md`)

As an intended reader, both exports read as genuine evidence audits, not advocacy. Each issue opens with a thesis that immediately narrows its own claim ("数据支持产出、收入和消费不同步，不直接证明全国居民'体感变差'" / "现有证据不足以断言有政治设局、情报泄露或出口许可被操纵"), then places a structured evidence review — records with 期间/口径/源头组, reproducible calculations, and claims tagged 证据支持 / 仅支持有限判断 / 证据不足 / 条件推演 — **before** any mechanism essays and conditional scenarios. The "情景" sections are explicitly "非概率预测" and gated on falsification conditions. The writing is unusually disciplined about the one failure mode the brief named: it refuses to let "消费较慢 → 信心弱 → 消费较慢" pass as an independent chain, and it refuses to count the congressional letter / board probe / lawsuit as three independent confirmations of one media-sourced identity claim. That is the correct call and it is stated plainly, not buried.

The cold read surfaces no claim that a reader would be misled into taking as established. Every material number is labeled as a公布值 with a single official original or an机构预测 with version, and each single-source figure carries an explicit non-extrapolation boundary.
