# 亲子旅行手册配图

使用内置 imagegen 工具生成，非 CLI。图片只表达旅行情境，不是景区实拍，也不是精确地图。

项目文件：`frontend/public/images/family-roadtrip.webp`（从生成原图转码压缩）。前端在“我的安排 → 查看图文行程”使用；只包含通用插画，不包含用户行程日期、个人资料或预算。私人行程仍从受认证保护的安排接口读取，未写入前端静态代码。

最终提示词：

> Use case: illustration-story. Create one high quality wide landscape editorial illustration, about 3:1 aspect ratio, for a Chinese parent-and-six-year-old-child self-drive itinerary in Guangde and Wuhu, Anhui, in November. Continuous cohesive scene with three adjacent naturally connected story areas from left to right: LEFT Guangde lush bamboo grove, calm lake, a winding paved path with safe railings, a small car on a road; CENTER cheerful colorful fairytale theme-park castle, gentle merry-go-round, one generic parent and a six-year-old child holding hands seen from behind; RIGHT contemporary museum building, quiet city park and the small car returning on a road. Style refined modern Chinese travel journal, charming hand painted gouache with clean shapes, fine paper grain, no frames, crisp legibility at small scale, daytime, warm humane inviting family outing, emerald and sage foliage, clear sky blue, touches of sunny yellow and muted coral, white clouds. Important: this is an illustrative journey scene, NOT a photograph or literal map, not an accurate depiction of any real park landmark. All three zones have equal visual interest, main objects near their zone centers so crops remain useful. No typography, no letters, no dates, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no panel borders. No mountains dominating, no giant text space, no dramatic dark lighting. Deliver only this scenic background artwork, not a UI mockup or poster.
